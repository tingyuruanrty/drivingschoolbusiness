Booking Requirements

1. Permit Required

A valid California learner’s permit is required for all driving lessons.

2. Test-Day Service

Please book your DMV road test appointment before booking test-day service with us.

3. Not Sure Which DMV?

You can take a regular lesson first. We can suggest suitable DMV locations based on your driving level and area.

4. Need Help Booking?

If online booking is difficult, we can guide you step by step or book it for you. Call or text us anytime.

5. Location Not Listed?

If your DMV location is not listed, please contact us. We may still be able to arrange service in nearby areas.
